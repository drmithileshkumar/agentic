3.9 Debugging & discovery

Tool

Use

sorry

Placeholder — admits the goal unproved (compiles, but marks the proof incomplete). Never in a final proof; critical signal in agent logs.

#check e

Shows the type of e — e.g. #check Nat.add_comm

#eval e

Evaluates e and prints the result — e.g. #eval 1 + 1

exact? / apply?

Search the library for a closing/applicable lemma

simp?

Suggests the minimal simp only [...]

#loogle, #leansearch, #statesearch

In-editor search of Mathlib by type/name/goal shape — e.g. #loogle _ * (_ ^ _)
